# Major Update: Voice Search, Quick Filters & Smart Navigation

**Published on:** November 17, 2026

We are excited to roll out a major set of accessibility and navigation improvements across **VisionPlay** to make finding and downloading accessible Android apps faster and easier than ever.

## What's New for You

* **Voice Search:** You can now search for apps and games hands-free using your voice directly from the search bar on supported browsers.
* **Quick Download Filters:** Instantly filter your search results by **All Apps** (sorted A–Z), **Direct APK Only**, or **Play Store Only**, and use the new **Clear / Hide Results** button to reset your view anytime.
* **1-Click Share & Broken Link Reporting:** Every app card now includes a **Share** button to easily send direct app links to friends, along with a quick **Report Broken Link** option if a download link ever stops working.
* **Smart Collapsible Store Menu:** Browse seamlessly between Home and all 6 categories from any page using our new accessible collapsible menu—which automatically hides the page you are already viewing.
* **Cleaner Layout & Screen Reader Enhancements:** All category pages, the homepage, and the footer now feature clearer headings, a live store overview bar, and improved mobile button alignment for TalkBack and screen reader users.

Thank you for using **VisionPlay**! Have an app suggestion or feedback? Feel free to reach out using the **Contact & Suggestions** link in the footer below.
