## VisionPlay - Vision Assistance
[Home](../)

Welcome to the Vision section. Here you can find smart AI-driven visual interpreters, live human assistance tools, and accessible navigation utilities designed to help you perceive and explore your environment.

> **Total Available:** 5 Vision Apps

---

### 1. Be My Eyes - Helping the blind
Connects blind and visually impaired individuals with sighted volunteers or advanced AI (Be My AI) for visual assistance through live video calls.

* [View on Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bemyeyes.bemyeyes)

---

### 2. Cash Reader: Bill Identifier
Instantly identifies banknotes and paper currency from a wide range of currencies worldwide using auditory announcements and vibration patterns.

* [View on Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hayman.cashreader)

---

### 3. Envision AI
An award-winning assistive app that uses artificial intelligence to read text, describe scenes, detect colors, scan barcodes, and recognize people.

* [View on Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.letsenvision.envisionai)

---

### 4. Lazarillo Accessible GPS
An accessible GPS navigation tool built for blind users, delivering detailed voice notifications about nearby places, street intersections, and transit stops.

* [View on Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lazarillo)

---

### 5. MANI (Mobile Aided Note Identifier)
The Reserve Bank of India's official offline application designed to help visually impaired individuals identify Indian currency denominations.

* [View on Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rbi.mani)
